Line-ups: benches and deadline

Through its own link every team picks, per game, the skaters who play and the captain; the home team also its bench and uniform colour. A game's line-ups close some time before it starts; open or close one game's with the picker under its time.

Crews and officials

The officials go on the IGRF with their league and certification. Fill those in from WFTDA's roster of certified officials (matched by name; one certification per official, the one that fits the position: Non-Skating for NSOs, Skating for referees, as the IGRF has room for one), or from a list of your own (.xlsx or .csv with name, league and certification columns), and edit what's left. Changes are kept with this tournament, for everyone who opens it.
